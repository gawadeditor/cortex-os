IMAGE PROMPT STRUCTURE SKILL
Derived from: GPT-Image2-Skill (wuyoscar/GPT-Image2-Skill)

This section supplements SECTION 11 and SECTION 11B above. Apply these rules
to every image_prompt you write. They reinforce and do not override the brand
constraints in SECTION 11B (warm palette, no cool blue, near-black background)
or the zone reservation requirements in SECTION 11C (CONCEPT_DRIVEN_MODE).

---

ORDERING — structure before goal

Build every image_prompt in this element sequence:
  1. SCENE/ENVIRONMENT  — background and setting context first
  2. SUBJECT            — the primary visual subject
  3. KEY DETAIL         — one specific material, texture, age marker, or physical condition
  4. CONSTRAINT         — at least two explicit inline negative clauses

This ordering differs from the [Primary subject], [Action/State], [Setting]...
format in SECTION 11. Apply this sequence instead for all prompts you write.

Example transformation:

  Before (subject-first):
    "Corroded bronze gear fragments with green-brown marine patina, partial wooden
     housing, visible precision gear teeth, isolated on a rough stone plinth in a
     shadowed neoclassical archival alcove, wide close-up, warm amber spotlight,
     burnt sienna background, dust motes and deep bronze shadow."

  After (scene-first):
    "Shadowed stone alcove with burnt sienna walls, amber spotlight from above,
     ancient corroded bronze mechanism at centre-right, green-brown marine patina
     and fractured wooden housing, no inscriptions, no modern elements, no ambient halo."

---

INLINE CONSTRAINT PLACEMENT

Do not rely on negative_constraints[] from the Handoff Brief alone. Every
image_prompt must contain at least two explicit negative clauses embedded
directly within the prompt string itself, placed at the end of the prompt.

Required negatives for all Hypogeum historical thumbnails:
  "no inscriptions" or "no text"
  "no modern elements" or "no contemporary objects"

Add further negatives specific to each candidate's visual risk:
  "no direct gaze"               — for any scene containing a human figure
  "no museum label"              — for any artifact-on-surface composition
  "no ambient halo or spotlight ring" — for all compositions (prevents product-shot isolation)
  "no institutional lighting"    — reinforcement of BGA constraint

These negatives are written inside image_prompt, after the positive description,
as a comma-separated final clause.

---

SINGLE HERO SUBJECT

One visually dominant subject per prompt. Do not enumerate multiple co-equal
objects as a comma list ("gears, tablet, scroll, column"). If supporting elements
appear, frame them as environmental context behind or around the single hero.

The hero subject is named once, explicitly, in the SUBJECT position (element 2).
Other objects appear only as texture or depth in the SCENE/ENVIRONMENT element.

---

QUALITY SIGNAL

For 16:9 main episode thumbnails: include "photorealistic, high detail" as the
final style phrase when the hero subject is a physical artifact where surface
texture is critical to the curiosity gap (e.g. gear teeth, patina, inscriptions
on metal or stone).

For 9:16 Shorts thumbnails: include "dramatic vertical composition, mobile-optimised"
as the final style phrase to signal vertical-fill intent to the image model.
