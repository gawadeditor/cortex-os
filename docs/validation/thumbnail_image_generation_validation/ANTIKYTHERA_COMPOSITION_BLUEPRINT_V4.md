## What changed vs. V3, and why (DNA-driven, not hand-tuned)

| Aspect | V3 (hand-authored) | V4 (DNA-derived) | Why |

| Headline zone width | "left 36–40%" | "left 30–38%" | `headline_zone_width_pct` default is `[30, 38]` — the canonical ceiling is slightly tighter than V3's hand-picked range, satisfying "slightly more controlled headline scale" without hardcoding a one-off reduction |

```
Premium archaeological investigative documentary YouTube thumbnail, 16:9 — a serious archaeological-mystery editorial poster, not a fantasy or adventure-game illustration. One complete composition with integrated headline typography and a controlled asymmetric eye path: headline first, the human discovery moment second, the mechanism third.

HEADLINE ZONE (left 30-38% of frame): render the exact two-line headline:

IT SHOULDN'T
EXIST

Both lines left-aligned, stacked with clear breathing room between them — the lines must not crowd each other. "IT SHOULDN'T" is monumental condensed serif lettering. "EXIST" is larger but stops well clear of the bottom frame edge. The letterforms are carved weathered stone with a muted stone-and-bronze base surface — NOT solid bright gold. Cracked, distressed texture. Subtle three-dimensional bevel. A restrained warm-gold highlight catches only the upper-left edge of each letter, as a thin accent light, not a fill color. Dark inner shadow. Restrained black drop shadow. Perfectly spelled, highly legible at 320x180, physically integrated into the scene's lighting rather than pasted on top. A wide near-black gap separates the headline zone from the doorway zone — the rightmost letter and the doorway's light must not touch or crowd each other.

DOORWAY + FIGURE ZONE (center-left, roughly 22-25% of frame, offset left of the vertical midline — not centered): a glowing ancient stone doorway, positioned off-axis, casts localized warm amber light down worn stone steps. The light is contained and does not blow out — it retains visible texture and edge detail even at its brightest point, and it does not spread across the frame. A single human silhouette, larger and clearer than a distant background figure, is caught mid-step at the threshold, one hand reaching toward the light as if just discovering it — an active discovery gesture, not a passive stance. Backlit, faceless, creating scale and narrative pull, clearly readable against the contained light.

MECHANISM ZONE (right foreground, roughly 35-40% of frame, partially cropped by the right frame edge): a corroded bronze Antikythera-like mechanism, mostly in shadow, with only a few large gear forms and one dial partially caught by rim light — the rest of the object recedes into near-black. This is a glimpse that implies scale and complexity, not a fully-lit, fully-detailed display piece — the mechanism should read as something half-glimpsed and mysterious, not as an object being presented for inspection. Dark verdigris-bronze patina (never turquoise). Warm gold rim lighting on only the nearest edges — the mechanism must NOT become the brightest object in the frame and must NOT dominate through visual density; the headline and the doorway light remain the brighter, busier reads.

Strong near-black separation between all three zones — deep shadow gaps keep the headline, the doorway/figure, and the mechanism visually distinct rather than fused into one block. Asymmetric composition, not a balanced rule-of-thirds grid. One coherent camera perspective, one physically continuous underground chamber, one warm directional light source anchored at the doorway. Restrained, investigative mood throughout — mystery and discovery, not spectacle or grandeur.

No extra text, no random lettering, no inscriptions, no symbols, no watermarks.
```

**Canonical DNA directive** (from `describe_dna_constraints(comp_plan)`):

```
 — THUMBNAIL COMPOSITION DNA: Deliberate visual hierarchy with a clear eye path from primary to secondary to tertiary read. The headline occupies roughly 30-38% of frame width — monumental but controlled, not consuming the composition. Strong near-black negative-space separation between every focal zone — no crowding, no fused blocks. The brightest light source stays localized and contained, never a full-frame wash, and never erases the readability of the human figure or hero subject near it. The hero/mystery object is clear and readable at a glance, not overloaded with fine detail. Composition must remain clearly readable at 320x180.
```
