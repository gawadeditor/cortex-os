How this works

Cortex publishes through Zernio. Connect each platform account (a YouTube channel, a TikTok account, …) in Zernio, run Platforms → Sync from Zernio, then bind the right account to {{ channel.name }} here. Each channel binds its own accounts — connecting a platform for one channel never affects another. The same account is bound for both publishing and analytics.
