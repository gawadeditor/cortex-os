How We Make Our Videos

AI-assisted short videos for The Hypogeum and Twinkle Mini Tales.

Every video on our channels is made with AI tools, under human direction. We say so openly: the images and clips are generated, the narration is a synthetic voice, and the stories are written with AI assistance and then checked against our own rules before anything is published.

1. Choosing a topic

We look at what our audience already watches to the end, then pick topics worth a short story: a real archaeological question for The Hypogeum, a gentle original tale for Twinkle Mini Tales. Each topic is scored before we spend anything on it.

2. Research and script

For documentary topics, claims are researched first and the script is built around what the sources support. The opening line is written to earn the next ten seconds. Children's stories are original, calm, and written to be fine to fall asleep to.

3. Voice, video and music

A synthetic narrator reads the script. The scenes are generated one by one with text-to-video models, then assembled with music and captions into a vertical video. A scene that does not meet our quality checks is regenerated, never repeated to fill time.

4. Checks before publishing

Each video passes format, length and cover checks. Videos for children are marked as made for kids. The designed cover image is uploaded by hand on YouTube, so a Short never goes live with a random frame.

5. Publishing and learning

We publish to YouTube, TikTok, Instagram and Facebook, then read how each video performed and feed that back into the next topics and openings.
